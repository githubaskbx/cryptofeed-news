CryptoFeed News (cryptofeed.news) — headlines, a hot list, and an AI daily brief

I shipped a crypto news product: CryptoFeed News / 加密沸点.

Live (official domain): https://cryptofeed.news

It pulls public reporting from industry outlets and project channels into one feed, and groups copies of the same story so you do not open every duplicate. A hot list ranks what spread over the last 24 hours. An AI daily brief closes the day in Chinese.

What you can open

  • Feed: https://cryptofeed.news/
  • Hot list (coverage and follow-up, not a price board): https://cryptofeed.news/hot
  • AI daily brief: https://cryptofeed.news/brief/today

A high rank means more sources picked the story up. It does not mean the underlying claim has been independently verified. Summaries are machine-written. For a figure, a rule, or a quote, go back to the original.

English-language support is in development.

Stack notes

  • Web front end on Cloudflare
  • API and Postgres for the feed, hot list, and brief
  • AI for translation, summaries, tags, and story grouping

# CryptoFeed News

Crypto headlines, a 24-hour hot list, and an AI daily brief.

**Live site:** https://cryptofeed.news

**CryptoFeed News (加密沸点)** gathers public crypto reporting into one feed, groups copies of the same story, and adds a 24-hour hot list plus an AI daily brief.

## What it does

- Collects public reporting from industry outlets and project channels
- Groups items that belong to the same story
- Ranks a hot list by how widely a story was covered and followed over the past 24 hours
- Publishes an AI daily brief in Chinese
- English-language support is in development

The hot list is not a price board. A high rank means more sources picked the story up. It does not mean the underlying claim has been independently verified.

## Stack notes

- Web app on Cloudflare
- API and Postgres for the feed, hot list, and brief
- AI for translation, summaries, tags, and story grouping

## Disclaimer

CryptoFeed News is for finding and understanding the news. It is not investment advice and it does not replace the original report. AI output can omit or mistranslate a line. Check amounts, regulation, and quotes against the source.

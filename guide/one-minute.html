One-minute pass

Use this when the timeline is repeating one event. The goal is to decide whether today needs more than a glance.

  1. Scan titles and sources on the feed. Same-story copies should already sit together.
  2. If something looks crowded, check the hot list. Rank is spread over 24 hours, not price.
  3. Close with the AI daily brief when you want the day in one Chinese summary.

Stop there unless a figure, a rule, or a quote needs the original article.

AI daily brief

The daily brief is a Chinese summary of the day. Translation, tags, and story grouping are done by AI, then shown with the underlying items.

Omissions and mistranslations happen. When a number, a regulation, or a direct quote matters, open the original report.

English-language support for the product is in development. The brief you can read today is still the Chinese edition.

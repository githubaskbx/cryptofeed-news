About

CryptoFeed News (加密沸点) collects public crypto reporting and lines up copies of the same story. The point is to see what happened without rereading one event twelve times.

The site currently publishes in Chinese. English-language support is in development. Browsing does not require an account.

What it is not

  • Not a price ticker and not a trading terminal.
  • Not investment, legal, or security advice.
  • Not a claim that a widely covered story has been independently verified.

Publisher: CryptoFeed News. Corrections: contact@cryptofeed.news.

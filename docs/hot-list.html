Hot list

The hot list looks at the past 24 hours and orders stories by how widely they were covered and how much follow-up they drew.

A row near the top means more sources stayed with that story. It does not mean the price moved, and it does not mean the underlying claim has been checked by this site.

How to use it

  1. Open the list when a story seems to be everywhere.
  2. Read the title and the spread, then decide if the original is worth opening.
  3. Treat the AI write-up as a map, then verify figures and quotes at the source.
